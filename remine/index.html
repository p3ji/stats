Remine

Data stories the Daily didn't tell

Statistics Canada releases far more data than it narrates. A Daily article reports the headline cut and a few subgroups, then links to a table holding thousands of series nobody writes about. Remine computes what the same data supports saying, and publishes what was left out. Every number here is computed from the source table by code — never written by a language model — and every claim carries its vector IDs and reference periods. Open Stats Lab is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Statistics Canada.

Source: Statistics Canada, reproduced under the Statistics Canada Open Licence. Analysis is not Statistics Canada's. Official data lives at statcan.gc.ca.
