South Asian Canadians
The largest visible minority group in Canada is South Asian Canadians, comprising approximately 7% of the population, with a total of about 1.9 million individuals. Following South Asians, Chinese Canadians and Black Canadians are the next largest groups, each representing around 4% of the population.
