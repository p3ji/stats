Does Income Affect Health in Canada?
Yes, income does affect health in Canada. Research indicates that higher income levels are associated with better health outcomes. Canadians in higher income brackets experience a 5-10 year life expectancy advantage compared to those in lower income groups. Factors such as income and social status, education, and social support networks significantly influence health. For instance, the health status improves at each step up the income and social hierarchy, with the healthiest populations being those in societies that are prosperous and have an equitable distribution of wealth. Additionally, the relationship between income inequality and health status has been reexamined, showing a negative relationship in many studies. However, the impact of income dynamics over time on health outcomes is still less understood.
